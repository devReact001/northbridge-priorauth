# MP-IMG-003 Shoulder MRI

## 1 Purpose and scope
This policy describes when Meridian Health Plan considers MRI of the shoulder without contrast medically necessary. It applies to CPT code 73221. Meridian Health Plan is a fictional payer created for a portfolio project, and this document is not clinical guidance.

## 2 Definitions
Conservative therapy for the shoulder means at least 6 weeks of treatment that combines two or more of the following: supervised physical therapy, anti-inflammatory or analgesic medication, activity modification, and a subacromial or glenohumeral injection.
Rotator cuff weakness means reduced strength on abduction or external rotation, or a positive drop-arm test or empty can (Jobe) test.

## 3.1 Suspected rotator cuff tear
Shoulder MRI is approved for a suspected rotator cuff tear when the examination shows rotator cuff weakness and pain persists after at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy. No trial of conservative therapy is required after acute traumatic onset when the patient cannot actively abduct the arm.

## 3.2 Labral tear or shoulder instability
Shoulder MRI is approved for a suspected labral tear when the patient has recurrent dislocation or subluxation, a positive apprehension test, or persistent pain or instability after a traumatic dislocation, and radiographs have been reviewed.

## 3.3 Adhesive capsulitis
MRI is not medically necessary to diagnose adhesive capsulitis (frozen shoulder), which is a clinical diagnosis based on limited active and passive range of motion. MRI may be approved when the examination also suggests a rotator cuff tear or when surgery is planned.

## 4 Exclusions
Shoulder MRI is not considered medically necessary in these situations:
- routine screening or imaging without documented examination findings
- shoulder pain of less than 6 weeks duration without trauma or weakness
- a repeat MRI within 12 months without a change in clinical status

## 5 Required documentation
The request must include laterality (right or left); range of motion and strength findings; results of special tests; the radiograph result; each conservative treatment tried, with dates, duration and response; the mechanism of injury if traumatic; and the CPT and ICD-10 codes.
